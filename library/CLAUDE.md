# Volt Library

A free, public, searchable directory of resources for people working where law meets technology: cybersecurity, data privacy and AI. Visitors browse and filter; anyone should be able to suggest a resource, and a curator reviews suggestions before they go live.

## Current state

- `index.html` is the site (plain HTML/CSS/JS, no build step). It fetches `data/resources.json`, so serve the folder (`python3 -m http.server`) rather than opening the file directly.
- `data/resources.json` (85 resources, sections, topics) is generated by `scripts/sync-notion.mjs`. Don't edit it by hand.
- `data/curation.json` holds what Notion lacks: section definitions and their Notion category names, topic mapping, excluded rows, and per-resource fallbacks (section, kind, topics, url, event) keyed by name. Notion values win; curation fills gaps. See README.md.
- Tests: `node --test scripts/sync-notion.test.mjs`. The first test asserts that bare Notion rows plus `curation.json` reproduce `resources.json` exactly; update `resources.json` via the sync, not by hand.
- Search, topic filter, section navigation (URL hash, e.g. `#jobs`) and auto-hiding of past events all work.
- The suggestion form and curator review queue were built for a Claude artifact's `window.claude` database. Outside that environment `window.claude` is undefined, so the form falls back to "copy your suggestion". It needs a real backend.

## Source of truth: Notion

The content comes from the owner's Notion workspace (database "Volt Library"):

- Resources database: data source `collection://1b5ba277-333f-4b91-af77-1aa173ac16b5` (fields: Name, Category relation, Tags, URL, Event Date, Location, Cost, Description, AI summary)
- Resource Categories database: `collection://d8c8de5d-7cb3-4b50-8c42-fb089690192d`
- Certifications lists (not yet imported): `collection://1c53db93-a20f-8055-903e-000bfe36d218` and nested databases under its three pages (Cybersecurity, Data Privacy, AI certifications)

## Data notes from the first import (Oct 29, 2026)

- Dropped 3 of 88 Notion rows: one blank row, a duplicate "Cyber SN", and an empty "Certifications" placeholder page.
- 27 entries had no section in Notion and were placed in the closest section (mostly Tools and Learning Center).
- Notion had working links for only 15 entries. 52 official homepage links were added from general knowledge and must be verified. 18 entries still have no link.
- Topic labels (Cybersecurity, Privacy, AI, General) were inferred for everything except the job boards.

## Known content problems to fix before launch

- AI coverage is almost empty (2 entries) although AI is a headline topic.
- 11 of 13 events are in the past; 2 have no date.
- No entry has a description.
- The "Privacy Books" Notion page repeats about 25 titles three times, uses a third party's Amazon affiliate links, and includes titles/authors that could not be matched to real books. Vet it before publishing.
- The Notion page still carries the original template creator's social links and disclaimer.
